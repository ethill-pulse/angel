## OI investigation (pulse venues)
Eric asked how OI works across venues in the clearstreet env. Findings: Binance futures (BINF/BINC) is the only REST-polled OI source (`rest/binance.rs`). It sends one request per `http_request_interval_ms` (BINF 5s, BINC 15s) and rotates through symbols. Bybit, OKX and Deribit stream OI over WS. HYPL, Kraken and spot venues don't support OI. Binance has no OI over WS (confirmed against the official connector), so we keep the poller. Gaps found: the `/fapi` path is hardcoded, which likely breaks BINC; there's no 429/418 backoff (the funding poller has one); the interval is shared with the funding/dropcopy pollers; and there's no subscription scope. Wrote the impl doc at `repos/pulse/tmp/binance-oi-poller-hardening.md`.
