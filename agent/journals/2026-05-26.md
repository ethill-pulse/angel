Key findings from the doc:
- **3-leg trade**: CSD↔client (Leg 1), CSD↔Cayman back-to-back (Leg 2), Cayman↔Street external hedge (Leg 3). Inter-affiliate margin exemption (CFTC foreign affiliate) = no bilateral margin between CSD and Cayman.

- **Funding rate**: Every 8h. CS sets its own rate (not mirrored from exchange). Formula = CS cost of capital + house position limits + 1-min TWAP of exchange rate over 8h, annualized. Accrues to equity balance (not instrument price). Monthly swap resets.
- **Pulse responsibilities**: (1) Create perp instruments → FACT; (2) Compute + publish funding rate to FACT every 8h; (3) Accrual architecture decision — Option 1: Pulse sends cash posting to Voyager every 8h; Option 2: Voyager computes accrual from raw rate; (4) Handle 5-year expiry event.
