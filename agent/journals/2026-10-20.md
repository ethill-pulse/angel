## Binance markPrice revert
Eric asked why markPrice was changed to a string. The only basis was the design agent's unverified recollection that old records return "". Reverted binance.yml, the fixture and the test. Lesson: don't let a design agent's "as I recall" venue claim through as a schema change; verify it or drop it.

## live testnet check of rates fetchers
Sent the exact requests our fetcher builds to Binance testnet and Hyperliquid testnet/mainnet. Found two bugs and fixed both (uncommitted on et.historical_rates_pr1):
1. Binance fundingRate treats startTime=0 as absent and returns the LATEST record, so fetch_first_rate_time returned the newest settlement. Now startTime=1. (My earlier "testnet history starts 2026-10-18" came from this bug; UM goes back to 2025-07.)
2. Hyperliquid mainnet history from 2023 settled at ~8h (gaps of 456/480/504 min) before going hourly. Dropped the hardcoded 1h interval in favour of fill_intervals_from_consecutive_ts; reverted the pub(crate) on HYPL_FUNDING_INTERVAL_MS.
Confirmed working: Binance paging is ascending from a nonzero startTime; endTime is inclusive (half-open via -1); limit>1000 is rejected; a bad symbol gives []. HYPL endTime is inclusive; HIP-3 xyz:TSLA works on mainnet; an unknown coin returns `null` (our validator turns that into an Err).
