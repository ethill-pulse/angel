## ~14:00 — Options pricing / Nikhil Slack thread

Nikhil Kulkarni raised a concern about OTC option expiry pricing: if Bob/Suley write a contract with an 8am ET fixing time, FACT doesn't have a BTC-USD price at 8am. He proposed hourly snap instruments (`CLST.SPOT.BTC-USD.0800`) modeled after BBG FX snaps.

Eric's response: the TWAP delivery mechanism already handles this. Pulse samples the Deribit index every 4 seconds for 30 minutes leading up to expiry and publishes the TWAP result to FACT via Kafka at expiry time. No point-in-time snapshot dependency; any expiry time is supported generically.

Nikhil's follow-up: won't Risk also need this info? Eric's answer: yes, they can listen to the Kafka topic directly. Quant (Anton) + Bob + Suley own getting Risk sign-off on the methodology. Eric's role is to build what he's told. Methodology is industry best-practice (mirrors Deribit).

**Key decisions confirmed in this thread:**
- TWAP delivery to FACT is the canonical approach — no hourly snap instruments needed
- Risk can consume the Kafka topic if they want the data
- Methodology sign-off is Anton/Bob/Suley's problem, not Eric's
- Nikhil is not fully looped in on the pricing design — pointer to the Notion doc sent
