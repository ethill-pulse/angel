## (session) funding-rate validator uses funding_interval_ms
- **Gap found:** funding_interval_ms (#4323) was only on the MarkPrice entries. The lastprice publisher's market_data_statistics output on mktdata.fundingrate dropped it.
- **Eric chose carry-through:**
  - Pulse main (uncommitted): optional funding_interval_ms added to the market_data_statistics entries schema (reuses custom tag 5026). The publisher copies it from the final funding-rate entry.
  - Verified: 60 tests pass, the whole-app cargo check passes, make lint exits 0.
- **Validator rewrite** (tools/funding-rate-validate/validate_funding_rates.py):
  - Each settlement's interval is the gap since the previous one, so the validator walks back from the newest settlement and projects forward at the newest interval, with a grace period.
  - Intervals must divide 24h and slots must be on the 00:00 UTC grid.
  - Rows without an interval fall back to the most common gap.
  - New flags: --hours, --grace-minutes, --all, --now. validate.sh passes extra args through.
  - Tested: on the old capture it reproduces the 9 symbols missing 15:00Z. On synthetic data it handles 2h, a 4h gap, an 8h→1h switch, a stale trailing feed, and an invalid 5h interval.
