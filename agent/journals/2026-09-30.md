## 18:10
Eric: we restarted many times today and only 15:00Z was affected, so it's not a generic startup bug. New finding: HYPL/BINF/CLST rows in mktdata.fundingrate are written by clearstreet-lastprice-publisher (markprice.rs), not the feed handler. It is a scaleset ScalingConsumer that seeks End on assignment (main.rs:95/135) and throttles each symbol to 60s (the source of the :33 batching). So a final funding rate the feed handler emits while the publisher is down or not yet assigned is lost, and the feed-handler watermark has already advanced, so nothing is re-sent until the next settlement. At 15:00 the topic was dark 15:00:26–15:03:33, and one pod's 28 HYPL symbols had their 15:00 emit or seed fall inside that gap; the other restarts ordered correctly. Proposed fix: the feed handler re-emits the latest settled rate on every poll, and the publisher already dedupes via published_funding_rate_ts. Exact ordering unconfirmed; the 15:00 logs are only in Datadog.
